05 – WORLD RECORDS THAT SEEM IMPOSSIBLE

The WCA currently has 17 official events, ranging from solving a 2×2 in under a second to solving a cube blindfolded,
finding a solution in as few moves as possible, and solving dozens of cubes from memory.

THE CLASSICS

  • 2x2x2

    • The tiny cube. No centers, no edges, just corners and people can solve it in under a second.
    Current World Record: 0.39 seconds

  • 3x3x3‼️

    • The cube may be familiar, but its fastest solves are anything but.
    Current World Record: 2.76 seconds

  • 4x4x4 → 7x7x7

    • The 4×4 average world record is currently 18.56 seconds,
    while the 7×7 single has been pushed down to around 1½ minutes.

THE OTHERS

  • 3x3 One-Handed🔥

    • Same cube. One hand.
    Current World Record: 5.62 seconds

  • Pyraminx

    • A pyramid-shaped puzzle that looks much simpler than it is.
    Current World Record: 0.73 seconds

  • Skewb

    • It doesn't even turn like a normal cube.
    And the world record single is also under a second.
    Current World Record: 0.73 seconds

THE INSANE EVENTS

  • 3×3 Blindfolded🔥

    • Look at a scrambled cube. Memorise it.
    Put on a blindfold. Solve it.
    Current World Record: 11.56 seconds

  • 4×4 & 5×5 Blindfolded

    • Because apparently solving a 3×3 blindfolded wasn't difficult enough.
    Current elite performances are around a minute and a half for 4×4 and under two minutes for 5×5 in single solves.

  • Multi-Blind🔥

    • Solve multiple cubes blindfolded.
    First, you inspect and memorize all of them.
    Then once the blindfold goes on, you solve them one by one.

    The current 3x3 multi-blind Rubik's cube world record belongs to Graham Siggins,
    who successfully solved 63 out of 65 cubes in 58 minutes and 23 seconds.

  • Fewest Moves

    • This one is especially cool because it's the opposite of speedcubing.
    You get a scramble. You get one hour.
    And instead of solving it as quickly as possible...you try to solve it in as few moves as possible.
    Current World Record: 16 moves
