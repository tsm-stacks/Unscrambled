01 – THE ACCIDENTAL PUZZLE

You'd be surprised to know that the Rubik's Cube wasn't originally created to be a puzzle at all.

Back in the 1970s, Ernó Rubik, a Hungarian professor, wanted to help his students understand 3D geometry.
To do so, he built a small cube whose pieces could rotate around different axes while still staying together.

Sounds simple enough, right? Well...after scrambling it, Ernó realised he couldn't put it back together.
What started as a teaching tool had somehow turned into a ridiculously complicated puzzle.
It took him about a month to figure out how to solve his own creation.
If you've never solved a Rubik's Cube before, trust me, trying to solve it on your own, without any external help is almost impossible.
So how do millions of people solve it today? We'll get to that soon.

Getting back to the story, he eventually patented it as the Magic Cube in Hungary.
When it went international, it was renamed the Rubik's Cube.
And that's the funny part, one of the most famous puzzles in the world today...was created by accident.

If you are curious, you'll find the puzzles around you.
